Who it helps

Microblading is for you if

  • Your brows were over-plucked years ago and never grew back
  • Your brows are sparse, light or uneven
  • You lost brow hair to alopecia
  • Your brows thinned after chemotherapy
  • You are tired of pencils, powders and gels

Your skin type, health history and medications all matter, so Kelsey checks fit at a consultation first. Is it right for your skin?

Healing

What healing looks like

  1. Days 1 to 3Strokes look crisp and darker than the final color. Apply the aftercare ointment as directed.
  2. Days 4 to 10Light flaking and itching. Don't pick or scratch. Let the flakes fall off on their own.
  3. Days 10 to 14Brows can look faded or patchy. The pigment is still there under new skin.
  4. Weeks 3 to 6The true color comes back and settles.
  5. Follow upKelsey checks your healing and perfects the shape.
